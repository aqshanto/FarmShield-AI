# FarmShield AI - Project Overview

## Project Identity

**Project Name:** FarmShield AI

**Theme:** NASA-powered Climate Intelligence Platform for Farmers

## Vision

Create an interactive, animated, and user-friendly web application that
transforms complex NASA Earth observation data into simple climate
insights for farmers.

The application should feel modern, engaging, and enjoyable while
solving a serious real-world problem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Problem Statement

Farmers face climate uncertainty:

-   Unexpected floods damage crops
-   Water shortages reduce production
-   Crop health problems are detected too late
-   Scientific environmental data is difficult for farmers to understand

The core problem:

Farmers need simple, visual, and early information to make better
decisions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Solution

FarmShield AI converts:

NASA Satellite Data ↓ AI Analysis ↓ Risk Detection ↓ Beautiful
Interactive Visualization ↓ Simple Farmer Recommendation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Product Experience Goal

The application should not look like a traditional data dashboard.

It should feel like:

-   Interactive
-   Animated
-   Friendly
-   Modern
-   Easy for non-technical users

Design inspiration:

-   Modern AI products
-   Interactive maps
-   Gamified experiences
-   Storytelling interfaces

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Features

## Climate Overview Dashboard

Shows:

-   Overall farm condition
-   Risk score
-   Weather summary
-   Recommendations

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Interactive Earth Map

Features:

-   Animated map
-   Satellite layers
-   Risk overlays
-   Smooth transitions
-   Location exploration

Layers:

-   Flood risk
-   Water stress
-   Crop health

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Flood Risk Monitoring

Uses:

-   Rainfall data
-   Soil moisture
-   Historical patterns

Output:

Flood risk level and preparation advice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Water Stress Detection

Uses:

-   Soil moisture
-   Temperature
-   Vegetation data

Output:

Water availability status and irrigation recommendation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Crop Health Monitoring

Uses:

-   NDVI
-   Vegetation indicators

Output:

Crop health score and action suggestions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## AI Farmer Assistant

Features:

-   Bengali support
-   English support
-   Simple explanations
-   Voice interaction capability
